## **Blockchain in Banking System**

The banking system, current banking system. The transactions made in the current banking system come with a number of issues and drawbacks. Consider an example where two people are doing a cross border payment. 1 person is based in the U. S. Let's call them John. And John's friend, Kat, is based in India now. John wants to transfer $1000 to cat in India. Currently, the way this process works is that John will go to his bank in the U. S and will give them $1000 with the instructions to transfer them to cat in India. Once John has done the needful, both John and Kat have to wait. And after about 2 to five days, and the amount of
$1000 will reflect in Katz bank account in India. Now let's. look at some of the issues with this process. Issues in banking system. Apart from the time that the process is taking, it has a lot of other issues. Firstly, the presence of intermediaries makes the process very expansive. Apart from the fees that is charged by every intermediary, banks must also incur costs for hedging to counter the volatility of currency. In fact, the global cross-border remittance fee is estimated to be $20 billion every year.
This amount is more than the G. D. P. Of some of the smaller countries in the world. Moreover, there are innumerable cases registered where hackers and fraudsters took over the control of the bank's online systems to siphon out funds worth millions of dollars, resulting in net frauds and account hacking. Lastly, situations like financial crises puts the money at risk if it's held by a 3rd party organization like a bank. Blockchain solution to the issues. Blockchain can overcome a number of these issues. Let's see how
So we already saw how the transaction between John and Kat gets executed through traditional banking channels. Now let's understand how the same transaction would take place if John and Kat were using Bitcoin, a cryptocurrency powered by blockchain. 1st John. first John uses his Bitcoin wallet to initiate a transaction. The amount is then sent to Katz's public address, which is the equivalent of a bank account number in the Bitcoin world. The amount thus gets credited almost instantaneously in Kat's wallet as opposed to taking two to 5 days like a bank does in the banking system. We saw that every bank maintains its own Ledger and thus the banks need to update them independently and reconcile periodically. The. blockchain on the contrary, comprises of a single Ledger shared among all participants. Thus, no separate messaging protocol is
required thus blockchain is tackling the issues in traditional banking world with
some of its features like decentralized system, distributed Ledger, incentives of validations, consensus algorithm and advanced cryptography in the coming modules. We'll look at each of these features in much more detail Thus, banks and other financial institutions have started looking into blockchain as they want to better understand and implement this technology for various other use cases
